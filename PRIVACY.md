# Privacy Policy

The canonical privacy policy for Lexora is published at:

**[https://lexora.sh/privacy](https://lexora.sh/privacy)**

The following is a summary of key privacy practices.

## Data Processed

Lexora may process user-selected text and related reading context (such as surrounding page text) in order to provide translation, explanation, pronunciation, and AI-assisted comprehension features.

## Provider Routing

Lexora routes requests to external services based on user configuration. Depending on your settings, selected text and reading context may be sent to:

- **Translation providers** — third-party translation APIs (e.g. DeepL, Google Translate)
- **Cloud AI providers** — cloud-hosted AI services for explanation and comprehension features
- **User-configured OpenAI-compatible endpoints** — custom API endpoints you configure yourself
- **Local AI runtimes** — on-device runtimes such as Ollama, where data does not leave your machine
- **Public knowledge sources** — services such as Wikipedia and Wikidata for definitions and context

The specific providers used depend on your Lexora configuration. Review the canonical privacy policy for current details on each provider and what data is transmitted.

## Data Sales

Lexora does not sell user data to third parties.

## Questions

For privacy-related questions, refer to [https://lexora.sh/privacy](https://lexora.sh/privacy) or contact [lexora.dev@proton.me](mailto:lexora.dev@proton.me).
